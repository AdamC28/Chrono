How to Play

To solve a Chrono puzzle, you must create a path that visits all of the tiles on the board once, without crossing itself.

Click a tile to add it to your path. The starting point is in light blue. Selected tiles are grey; your last selected tile is dark blue.

You can only select tiles if they satisfy these rules:

  • The tile must be adjacent (including diagonally) to your previously selected tile.
  • For every two tiles, the number of the following (third) tile must be the result of a a valid mathematical equation between the first two.
    • The first two tiles must complete an equation with the starting point's number. The result, on the second tile you select, is now the first term in a new operation you must complete with two other tiles, and so on...
    • A valid mathematical equation involves either addition, subtraction, multiplication or integer division (the +, -, x, or / operators).
